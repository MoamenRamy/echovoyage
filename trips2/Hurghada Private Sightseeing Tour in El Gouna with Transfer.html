About

Discover El Gouna from land and sea on this half-day tour from Hurghada. Cruise through the lagoon on a sailing trip, explore the Abu-Tig Marina with a guide, and visit the El Gouna Tower.

Feast your eyes on panoramic views of the El Gouna cityscape from the water Learn more about the history and culture of the city from your private guide Feel like a local as you sail through the lagoon in a traditional Egyptian boat Haggle for a bargain with local vendors at the Abu-Tig Marina market stalls See the city from El Gouna's tallest building at the top of the El Gouna Tower

What to expect

Itinerary

Stop At: Hurghada, Hurghada, Red Sea and Sinai
After picking up all comers, the bus will head to the resort city of El-Gouna. It takes approximately 40 minutes to get to it.

Duration: 30 minutes


Stop At: El Gouna, El Gouna, Hurghada, Red Sea and Sinai
The beginning of the tour will be a lovely one – a lagoon trip. This lagoon trip will last one hour. Of course, you can enjoy so much this lagoon trip by speedboat as it will allow you to discover the place from the sea. It will be something outstanding! At the next stop, you will be making a panoramic overview of the city. Following this panoramic view of El-Gouna, you will drive to the Down Town of the city. After that, you will have a walk in the Abu-Tig Marina. Later, you can go shopping in the place. we will visit El-Gouna tower the highest place in El-Gouna to see El-Gouna from a high place to watch golf playgrounds, see hotels and take nice pictures.

Duration: 4 hours


Stop At: Hurghada, Hurghada, Red Sea and Sinai
Take in views of the golf courses and hotels from the top of the tower before returning to your hotel in Hurghada in the afternoon.

Duration: 30 minutes
