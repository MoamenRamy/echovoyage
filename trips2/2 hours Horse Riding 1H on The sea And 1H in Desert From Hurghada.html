About

Combine a wildlife encounter with a chance to explore the great natural wonders of Hurghada on this horseback riding adventure. Access terrain that would take a long time to explore on foot—such as Hurghada's vast desert area. A highlight is being able to ride your horse through the sea. No need to worry if you're a beginner, as helmets are provided and a trainer is on hand to coach you and provide assistance if you need it.

What to expect

Itinerary

This is one of the most exotic destinations for horseback riding in Egypt, and to top it off, it is reasonably priced!

The ancient horse culture is very much alive in Egypt, and it is doubtful that any part of the world can match its rich and broad mosaic of history.

Take this horseback tour with the finest bred horses and experience trainers.

Horse trainers are also provided to give you pre-ride coaching, and to assist you throughout your ride.

Another must-do excursion activity while in Hurghada, for horse lovers of all kinds! This is a family friendly activity.

    Traveler safety:

  • This tour is child appropriate
  • A helmet is provided to each rider
  • Pregnant women are not permitted to ride a horse
  • A disclaimer form against damage and personal injury required to be signed prior to the tour
  • Horse at both desert and riding by the beach
Additional information
  • Confirmation will be received at time of booking
  • Most travelers can participate
  • This experience requires good weather. If it’s canceled due to poor weather, you’ll be offered a different date or a full refund
  • This tour/activity will have a maximum of 30 travelers
