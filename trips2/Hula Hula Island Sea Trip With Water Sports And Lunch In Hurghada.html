About

Spend a full day off the coast of Hurghada and combine several cultural experiences—like belly dancing performances and folkloric shows—in one convenient excursion. Don’t worry about the logistics of planning your own multi-attraction tour, as everything is organized for you. Instead, spend time snorkeling, enjoying the Floating Aqua Park, and exploring Paradise Island for a day of adventure.

What to expect

Itinerary

Have a water sport adventure on Paradise Island and refuel with a delicious buffet lunch on this day tour in Hurghada’s Red Sea. This is a group tour, so you’ll share the experience, make a few new friends, but still have the freedom to enjoy the day doing whichever activities you choose.

Your tour starts with convenient hotel pickup. Then you’ll get to the pier, board your boat, and cruise out to Paradise Island. You’ll stop at a few different reefs to swim, snorkel, and explore (snorkel gear and life jackets are provided). After your delicious lunch, there will be time for riding banana or sofa boats if you like.

When the day is over, you’ll be transferred back to your hotel.
