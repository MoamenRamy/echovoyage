About

When you have the water at your feet, you have an unrivalled sensation of freedom! It's an exciting and adrenaline-filled exercise since you can continually take in privileged panoramic vistas. In the aquatic sport of parasailing, a harness and a number of ropes are used to suspend one or more people from a parachute while being pulled by a boat. Itinerary

Enjoy an exceptional vacation in Hurghada with parasailing, where a parachute is attached speedboat, one or two persons may ride in the same time, with an attractive view of this stunning location ascending up to 40-50 meters (about 164 feet) in the air. Even the most mild-mannered landlubber can soar to the heavens effortlessly and float hundreds of feet above the water, enjoying the kind of views and serenity that was once the domain of seagulls and eagles. The beauty of parasailing is that it requires no skill whatsoever; it is perhaps the easiest and gentlest thrill-seeker activity of all. After 10 to 15 minutes aloft, alone or with a companion, you’ll return to earth feeling a wonderful cocktail of calmness and adrenaline coursing through your veins.
