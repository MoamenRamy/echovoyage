What to expect

Itinerary

Stop At: Hurghada, Hurghada, Red Sea and Sinai
Embark on an unforgettable journey from Hurghada as we pick you up at 11 PM from your hotel in a comfortable private car with air-conditioning. Enjoy a scenic 4-hour drive to Luxor, where a delicious breakfast awaits you at a local restaurant. The hot air balloon company will pick you up from the restaurant at 4:45 AM and transport you to the balloon flight location. There, experienced professionals will provide thorough safety instructions to ensure a secure and enjoyable experience as you prepare for your breathtaking sunrise flight over Luxor's ancient wonders. Upon landing, your expert guide will be waiting to lead you on the next chapter of your adventure. Get ready to immerse yourself in the beauty and history of Luxor while enjoying every moment of your experience.

Duration: 4 hours


Stop At: Sunrise Ballooning Luxor Hot Air Balloon, Television, Gazirat Al Awameyah, Luxor, Luxor Governorate 85111, Egypt
Embark on a magical hot air balloon ride in Luxor, starting at 5 am for a sunrise flight. Witness the city's beauty from above as the sun illuminates ancient temples and the Nile River. Experience tranquility and awe as you glide through the early morning sky, creating memories that will last a lifetime.

Duration: 1 hour


Stop At: Great Pyramid of Cheops (Khufu), Giza Egypt
It is the oldest and largest of the three pyramids of Giza plateau and the only one to remain intact from the 7 Wonders of the Ancient World. The height of the great pyramid is 147m, the number of stones used in construction is 2,300,000 and it took twenty years to be built.

Duration: 30 minutes


Stop At: Valley of the Kings, Luxor 1413101 Egypt
Following your breathtaking hot air balloon ride over Luxor at sunrise, your adventure continues with a visit to the iconic Valley of the Kings. Explore the ancient burial grounds of pharaohs and marvel at the intricate tombs that have stood the test of time. Immerse yourself in the rich history and grandeur of this archaeological site, where the mysteries of Egypt's past come to life before your eyes.

Duration: 2 hours


Stop At: Temple of Hatshepsut, King Valley Road, Luxor Egypt
After your visit to the Valley of the Kings, your journey through ancient Egypt will lead you to the magnificent Hatshepsut Temple and the towering Colossi of Memnon. Explore the grandeur of Queen Hatshepsut's mortuary temple, with its impressive architecture and stunning reliefs that tell the story of this powerful female pharaoh.

Duration: 1 hour


Stop At: Colossi of Memnon, Thebes, Luxor Egypt
Next, marvel at the massive statues of the Colossi of Memnon, guarding the entrance to what was once the largest mortuary temple in the Theban necropolis. These towering figures stand as silent sentinels, offering a glimpse into the grandeur and scale of ancient Egyptian architecture and artistry. As you wander through these historic sites, you will be transported back in time to an era of pharaohs, gods, and monumental construction, gaining a deeper understanding of Egypt's rich and fascinating past.

Duration: 20 minutes


Stop At: West Bank, Luxor Egypt
Savor a delicious lunch and cruise across the Nile on a Felucca to the East bank. Immerse yourself in the beauty of the river and the charm of ancient Egypt on this unforgettable journey. Join us for a day of discovery and delight

Duration: 2 hours


Stop At: Luxor Temple, Luxor 23512 Egypt
Step into the grandeur of Luxor Temple, an ancient marvel that embodies Egypt's rich history and architectural brilliance. Dedicated to the god Amun, this temple is a captivating fusion of pharaonic and Greco-Roman styles, adorned with intricate hieroglyphics, towering columns, and majestic statues. Explore the temple complex and be mesmerized by the colossal statues of Ramses II and the well-preserved Avenue of Sphinxes. Luxor Temple is a testament to Egypt's enduring legacy, waiting to be discovered and admired by all who visit.

Duration: 2 hours


Stop At: Temple of Karnak, Karnak, Luxor Egypt
Picture this: as you step into the ancient grandeur of Karnak Temple, you'll be greeted by towering columns, intricate hieroglyphics, and colossal statues that whisper tales of a glorious past. Explore the sacred precincts dedicated to gods and walk the mystical Avenue of Sphinxes, unraveling the mysteries of Egypt's fascinating history in this majestic site.

Duration: 2 hours


Stop At: Luxor, Luxor, Nile River Valley
After exploring the magnificent Karnak Temple and Luxor Temple, your adventure draws to a close around 3 pm as we begin our journey back to Hurghada. Relax and enjoy the scenic drive, arriving safely at your hotel with memories of an unforgettable experience that will stay with you forever. Thank you for joining us on this incredible journey through ancient Egypt.

Duration: 2 hours
