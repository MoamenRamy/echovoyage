About

Take a quick tour of Hurghada's traditional old city and contemporary city. You will be picked up from your hotel and driven in an air-conditioned vehicle to Hurghada's biggest mosque. The primary religion in Egypt is Islam, which you can read more about here. Then we go to the marina, where there are lots of eateries and cafes, and we take in the fresh air and sea.

Before being dropped off at your hotel, we will then visit the Hurghada Handicrafts Museum and the Papyrus Museum where you can watch how Egyptian handicrafts and papyrus are made.

Itinerary

Stop At: Mosque El Mina Masjid, El Shohada, Hurghada Egypt
El Mina Mosque is one of the most famous mosques in Hurghada. It was opened in 2012 and was built on an area of ​​8,000 meters at a cost of 20 million pounds. It is located directly on the sea and can accommodate about 10,000 worshipers. It includes two high minarets, 25 domes, an event hall and another for lectures.

Duration: 45 minutes


Stop At: Hurghada Marina, Saqala Square, Hurghada 84511 Egypt
Yacht port surrounded by a shop-lined boardwalk featuring restaurants and bars with outdoor seating

Duration: 45 minutes


Stop At: Hurghada, Hurghada, Red Sea and Sinai
We will visit the Museum of Handicrafts and the Papyrus Museum in Hurghada to enjoy watching the way Egyptian handicrafts are made and the way papyrus is made.

Duration: 1 hour
