What to expect

Itinerary

We pick you up in a car from your hotel and drive you to the pier where our opulent speed boat is docked to begin our trip.

We then put you onto the speed boat and set off swiftly for The Dolphin House in the middle of the Sea so that you can experience the air flow and water impact, enjoying the fresh fruit, soft drinks, and refreshments that are offered aboard the speed boat.

You will experience some of the most unforgettable moments of your life snorkeling privately at one of the Red Sea's top snorkeling spots.

You will experience some of the most unforgettable moments of your life snorkeling privately at one of the Red Sea's top snorkeling spots. You will snorkel close to the area's magnificent coral reefs and countless colored fish even if dolphins aren't visible. You will participate in some aquatic activities following your snorkelling adventure. After you get back to the starting point, the transportation vehicles will be there to drive you to your accommodation.
