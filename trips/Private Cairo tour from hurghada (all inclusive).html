Itinerary

Pass By: The Museum of Egyptian Antiquities, Midan El Tahrir Geographical Society Building, Cairo 11511 Egypt
The museum displays a rare collection of 5000 years of art. Over 250,000 natural artifacts are presented, including an exhibit dedicated to Tutankhamen collection of treasure, gold, and jewelry which was enclosed in his tomb for over 3,500 years.

Duration: 1 hour 30 minutes


Stop At: Pyramids of Giza, Al Haram Str., Giza 12611 Egypt
The complex includes three main pyramids, six little pyramids, the great sphinx and the Valley Temple. The complex dates back to the 4th dynasty 2400 years B.C.

Duration: 30 minutes


Stop At: Great Pyramid of Cheops (Khufu), Giza Egypt
It is the oldest and largest of the three pyramids of Giza plateau and the only one to remain intact from the 7 Wonders of the Ancient World. The height of the great pyramid is 147m, the number of stones used in construction is 2,300,000 and it took twenty years to be built.

Duration: 30 minutes


Stop At: Great Sphinx, Al-Haram, Giza 3512201 Egypt
It is the largest statue in the world, standing 73 m. long, 19 m. wide, and 20 m in height. It is the oldest known monumental sculpture.

Duration: 30 minutes


Stop At: Pyramids of Giza, Al Haram Str., Giza 12611 Egypt
The Valley Temple: A Granite mortuary temple used by Ancient Egyptians for the mummification process and this is the only remaining one at Giza plateau.

Duration: 30 minutes
