About

Seize the opportunity to view the Red Sea’s underwater world without getting wet on this submarine experience in Hurghada. Ideal for families and anyone who doesn’t swim, the adventure takes you 82 feet (25 meters) beneath the waves in a modern, 44-passenger submarine with 18 panoramic windows. Step aboard and then sit back and gaze at the marine world, alive with swaying corals, exotic reef fish, and other colorful sea life.

Itinerary

Pass By: Sindbad Submarines, 1 Sindbad Club، 1 Mohamed Said St، Touristic Villages, Hurghada 1, Red Sea Governorate 84511, Egypt
The only real submarines in Africa. There are only 14 real recreational submarines worldwide, and two are right here in Hurghada, Egypt. A new adventure awaits you 25 meters below the sea. Join us for an unforgettable journey aboard the Sindbad Submarines, where you will explore 500 meters of coral reef and its marine inhabitants. Enjoy the breath-taking view in the air-conditioned comfort and safety of our submarine. It offers 44 passenger seats, two pilots' seats, and a sizeable round viewing window for each passenger. Experience the beauty of the Red Sea's underwater world without getting wet. It's perfect for all ages, and the memories will last a lifetime. We provide point-to-point transfer from your hotel to our terminal, including land-sea transportation, and all with an excellent level of service.


Pass By: Sindbad Aqua Park, 1 Mohamed Said St Sindbad Club, Hurghada 84511 Egypt
You will reach the submarine through a transfer boat and, during this time, you can admire the coast of the city of Hurghada while listening to good music. On your way back, the staff will offer you a juice, and you will receive a certificate of participation in this truly unique experience. We organize five trips per day (9:00 - 10:00 - 11:00 - 12:00 - 13:00), but the best time is in the morning when the water's visibility is the best. So join us for an unforgettable journey where you can experience all the colorful fish and marine life up close without even getting wet.


Pass By: Dive Base Academy, Serry Beach Resort, Village Road, القرى السياحية، الغردقة، محافظة البحر الأحمر 84511, Egypt
There is an entire underwater world just waiting for you to explore, and you may be itching to do so but are wary about the feeling of breathing underwater with scuba diving. In that case, an INTRO DIVE can prove to be the perfect introduction to exploring marine life, offering a fun way to gain basic skills and knowledge if you’re not ready for a complete course.
