About
Experience the top sights in Cairo with this full-day tour with flights included. On this adventure, you'll be immersed in Egyptian history, starting with a visit to the Museum of Egyptian Antiquities, home to King Tut's coffin, before heading to the Pyramids of Giza. After lunch on the Nile, jet back to Hurghada.

What to expect

Itinerary

Pass By: Hurghada, Hurghada, Red Sea and Sinai
Around 4am you will be picked up from your hotel. Upon arrival in Cairo, you will be met by your guide who will accompany you throughout the tour.
Stop At: The Museum of Egyptian Antiquities, Midan El Tahrir Geographical Society Building, Cairo 11511 Egypt
The excursion begins with a visit to the Egyptian Museum, which, in addition to its more than 120,000 exhibits, also houses Tutankhamun's golden coffin. Afterwards enjoy your lunch.

Duration: 2 hours 20 minutes


Stop At: Pyramids of Giza, Al Haram Str., Giza 12611 Egypt
The further course of the excursion takes you to the great pyramids of Cheops, Chephren and Mykerinus. The tour of the pyramids ends at the Sphinx and the temple surrounding it. At the end of the day, if time permits, you will have the opportunity to go shopping before embarking on the return journey to Hurghada.

Duration: 3 hours


Stop At: Nile River, Cairo 15662 Egypt
Lunch on the Nile

Duration: 1 hour


Stop At: Khan Al-Khalili, El-Gamaleya El Gamaliya, Cairo Egypt
If the return flight from Cairo is late in the evening, we will spend some time visiting the London of the Middle Ages (Khan el Klalili market), Old Cairo, recently added to the UNESCO World Heritage list, or Cairo Festival City, an extra-large shopping mall (air-conditioned), one of the largest in the Middle East. Full of the most famous international brands, restaurants, cafes and a huge colorful dancing fountain, where you will be drawn to Cairo's chic, modern nightlife.

Duration: 1 hour


Pass By: Hurghada, Hurghada, Red Sea and Sinai
Upon arrival at the airport, you will be transferred to your hotel in an air-conditioned car.
