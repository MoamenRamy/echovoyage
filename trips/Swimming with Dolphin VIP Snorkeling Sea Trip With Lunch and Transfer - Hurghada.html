What to expect

Itinerary

After you are picked-up from your hotel, transfer to a boat docked on the Red Sea. Cruise out to Dolphin House for one hour to uncover the underwater treasures of the reefs. Grab a snorkel and explore an alien habitat beneath the waves as you marvel at the colors and variety of the sea life. Relax with a delicious lunch, freshly prepared and served onboard. The Dolphin House is undoubtedly the most popular destination of all our Red Sea trips. There’s a great chance you will spot a lot of dolphins, but a success rate cannot be guaranteed. Jump off the boat at various spots to go snorkeling and glimpse the vast array of life this delicate habitat supports. Admire the dazzling colors, odd shapes, and psychedelic patterns as you observe the fish, coral, and plants. Make the most of your day in one of the world’s most popular and well-known snorkeling and diving sites Enjoy the Water Sports “Banana & Quadra” for 20 minutes

Finally, as your day winds down, you will be transferred back to land and returned to your hotel.
